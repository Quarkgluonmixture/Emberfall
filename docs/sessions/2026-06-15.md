### Vertical town walls — regenerated art + continuous-strip render

Owner reported the side (N-S) town walls read as discontinuous. Diagnosis
(headless geometry dump): not real gaps — the old `wall_vertical` art (28×85)
overlapped ~2.6×, but it was a tall thin pillar with a per-piece cap, so a
stacked column read as a row of separate posts vs the chunky continuous
horizontal runs. Owner regenerated the art (GPT-Image, a few iterations: angled
panels with a top "crown" stair-stepped/posted; the winning version is a
**straight-vertical** continuous strip — see ASSET_PROMPTS batch 11, and the key
insight that a *skewed* strip can't tile when stacked straight down).

New art (28×191) dropped into `assets_src/raw/11/`, `process-assets` re-sliced.
Render change (cosmetic): `PiecePlacement.h` added so a piece can scale its
height independently of width; `wallRect` now renders the dedicated N-S art as
ONE continuous strip per side (width = horizontal-run heft, height = side span,
centered, tucked under the corner towers) instead of stacking copies — which
fixes both the picket look and the overshoot the tall strip would cause if
stacked. Fallback (no dedicated art) still chains towers/log-clumps.
Verified: typecheck · 64/64 (incl. cluster engine) · lint · battery QA green ·
11×/13× close-ups show continuous walls from corner to corner at every town size.

Follow-up (owner: "some horizontal walls still not connected"): the wall ring's
`buildable` veto rejected BOTH ocean and river tiles, so on riverside towns the
top/bottom run lost pieces where it crossed the river — intermittent gaps that
read as a defect (inland towns like Ravenstead were already fully closed with a
gatehouse). Fix: a separate `wallBuildable` predicate (threaded layoutCluster →
wallRect) that vetoes only Ocean, so walls span rivers and keep the ring closed
while still opening to the open sea; buildings keep the stricter ocean+river
veto. Riverside towns (Briarcairn, Fernfell) now close across the river. The
remaining south-center opening is the intentional town entrance (stone towns
fill it with a gatehouse; palisade towns leave it open).

Follow-up 2 (owner: "shouldn't it be cell-by-cell, overlapping, not one strip
pulled down?"): right — the single stretched strip read flat. Reverted to
STACKING overlapping cells (front occludes back → coursed wall with 3/4 depth,
matching the horizontal run). Since the regenerated art is one long continuous
strip (28×191), the pipeline now crops a single tileable CELL from it (folder
11: skip the crenellated top, keep a ~28×62 body band) and `wallRect` stacks
that cell down each side at ~50% overlap. The `PiecePlacement.h` stretch path
is retained but no longer used for walls.

Follow-up 3 (gpt-5.5 art critique via codex, `docs/playtest/CODEX_WALLS_CITIZENS.md`):
owner + codex confirmed the walls/citizens still read rough — horizontal runs are

No-art down payment now: corner towers draw on top (`PiecePlacement.layer`, sort
by layer then dy) and wall runs tuck under them (`tuck`), so the ring reads as
joined. Owner is regenerating clean horizontal + modular vertical wall art

tuning, citizen variant slicing + selection) lands when that art arrives.
